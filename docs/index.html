Common questions

Do I need an account? No. GigGrow has no sign-up and no password. Open it and start logging.

Where is my data? On your iPhone. If you're signed in to iCloud it also syncs through your own private iCloud database so it reaches your other devices — see the privacy policy.

Does it connect to Uber or Lyft? No. GigGrow never asks for your gig-app logins. It reads your earnings from a screenshot you take, on the device.

Why are the daily amounts marked ≈? A weekly summary screen gives an exact total but only draws the days as bars. GigGrow measures the bars to split the week, so the weekly figure is exact and the daily ones are close.

How do I get my data out? Settings → Back up writes a complete JSON file. Settings → Export writes a CSV for a spreadsheet or an accountant.

How do I delete everything? Settings → Erase all data. With iCloud on, that reaches your other devices too.
